Information you give us

Technical information

When you visit the site, our hosting and technical service providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and similar data needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

When you submit a build request, we may also receive related technical and session context that helps us review the enquiry and reduce spam. Depending on your browser settings, this may include items such as:

  • an anonymous visitor identifier stored in your browser
  • visit count and basic visit timing on the same device
  • landing page URL and first-touch UTM parameters, if present
  • session duration, scroll depth, and whether you reached the apply section
  • approximate location derived from network headers provided by our hosting platform
  • referral information and similar request metadata

This site also uses Vercel Web Analytics for aggregated usage insights. It is not used as an advertising pixel.

This site uses Microsoft Clarity to help us understand how people use the website. Clarity may collect usage data such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolls, and session recordings or heatmaps. Clarity is used to improve the site and is not used by us as an advertising or retargeting pixel.

Cookies and similar technologies

At the time of this policy, DW Funnel does not intentionally use advertising or retargeting cookies on this website.

The website may use technical storage and analytics tools required for security, functionality, form handling, site operation, and understanding site usage. In particular, the site may use:

  • localStorage for an anonymous visitor ID and related first-touch visit context used with build-request submissions
  • sessionStorage for temporary form draft data and short-lived session metrics while you complete a build request
  • hosting and analytics infrastructure that may set cookies or similar storage as part of delivering the site
  • Microsoft Clarity, which may use cookies or similar technologies for session analytics, heatmaps, and recordings

If you are in a region where browser storage identifiers or analytics cookies require consent, you can clear or block this storage at any time through your browser settings. The site remains usable without it.

If we introduce advertising pixels or other marketing tracking technologies in the future, we may update this policy and introduce consent controls where required.
