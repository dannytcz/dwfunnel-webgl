Who we are

DW Funnel is a funnel design studio. This policy explains what we collect when you visit this site or contact us, and what we do with it.

When you book a call or message us, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, contact details, and information about your business and offer. Bookings are processed by cal.com and messages by WhatsApp; both operate under their own privacy policies. This site does not run advertising trackers.

We use your information to run the fit call, scope and deliver your build, and follow up about your enquiry. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties beyond the tools named above.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to serve you. You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us through the channels on the main page.
