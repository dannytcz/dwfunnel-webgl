        Need to know

        Questions, answered.

        Which Lexis shoe is right for me?

        Choose Velocity X1 for versatile speed, Tempo Carbon for race day, Drift Daily for high-cushion training, and Trail Vector for mixed or technical ground.

        Can I really run in the shoes before returning them?

        Yes. The 30-day performance trial is designed for real miles. Keep the original packaging and start the return within 30 days of delivery.

        How should the Velocity X1 fit?

        Expect a secure midfoot, comfortable heel hold, and roughly a thumb’s width of space at the toe. It runs true to standard US sizing.

        How long does delivery take?

        Orders dispatch in 1 to 2 business days. Typical delivery is 2 to 5 business days domestically and 5 to 10 days internationally.

        How do I care for Lexis shoes?

        Remove surface dirt with a soft brush, hand wash with cool water and mild soap, then air dry. Do not machine wash or tumble dry.
