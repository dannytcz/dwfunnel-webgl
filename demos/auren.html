The House of AUREN

Since 1902, a single AUREN atelier in the Vallee de Joux has shaped raw metal into instruments of time. Every bridge is chamfered by hand, every jewel set under the loupe, the slow arithmetic of patience.

We do not measure a movement in components, but in the hours a master spends coaxing it toward silence. What leaves our workshop is not a product. It is an inheritance.
