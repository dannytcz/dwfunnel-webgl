The vision

A kitchen that cooks with restraint.

Chef Alexander Thorne opened The Brew on the belief that luxury should never need to announce itself. Find the best expression of an ingredient, then remove everything that gets in its way.

Raised between coastal kitchens and European dining rooms, Thorne brings fire-led technique and a tightly seasonal point of view to every service.
