010 / Intel

Asked before the call.

Everything buyers usually want to know before booking. If yours is not here, bring it to the call.

Q.01What does a build cost?

Every build is scoped on the fit call. Flagship cinematic builds are a five figure engagement; platform builds on your existing stack start in the four figures. A full proposal follows the call: scope, price, and timeline in writing.

Q.02How long from brief to launch?

Fifteen days on a typical build: two for discovery, three for strategy, three for design, five to seven for the build, and two for QA and launch. You see progress at every phase.

Q.03Which platforms do you build on?

GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, Webflow, Framer, Shopify, Kajabi, WordPress, and fully custom code, like this page. We recommend the stack that fits your operation, not the one that fits our habits.

Q.04Who writes the copy?

We do. Conversion copy is the spine of every build, drawn from your offer, your buyer conversations, and your proof. You review and approve every line before launch.

Q.05Do I own everything?

Yes. The page, the assets, the tracking, and the automations all live on your accounts from day one. If we part ways, everything keeps running without us.

Q.06I already have a page. Rebuild or repair?

We audit it on the fit call. If the structure is sound we repair and rewire; if the foundation leaks we rebuild. Either way the decision comes with the reasoning, not just the invoice.
